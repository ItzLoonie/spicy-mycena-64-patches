# Loonie's Spicy Patches


## Area Randomizer Tracker
This patch adds a new section to the Level Items screen.\
This new section is a full tracker for area rando.

Supports all form of entrance randomization.

## Auto Collect Coins
This patch will automatically collect coins that are produced from objects like enemies and coin boxes.

## Better Level Items Screen
This patch tweaks the Level Items screen to be easier to understand what you have and don't have at a glance.\
This is accomplished by making missing items gray instead of the Y/N system.

## Clean AP Notifications
This patch overhauls the appearance of Archipelago item notifications, making them more readable and support to show more than one at a time.\
You will also be told what course was affected by an Uncollect Random Coin Trap.\
Item tiers are also reflected.

## Easy Airborne Coins
This patch makes collecting coins and star secrets easier when flying with the Wing Cap or when being fired from a Cannon.

## Easy Snowball
This patch makes the Snowman Lost His Head star easier by removing the need to meet requirements for it to succeed.\
Instead, it will always succeed.

## Item HUD
This patch adds a key counter, a move overlay and a global coin counter to the HUD.

## Let Me Cheat
This patch disables the cheat detection for Koopa the Quick and the Big Penguin, allowing you to use cannons and take the secret tunnel.

## Random Instruments
This patch adds three new Sound options which allows you to toggle whether or not instruments are random for music, jingles and sound effects.

> [!WARNING]
> This patch will be deprecated if and when Spicy Mycena 64 receives Archipelago options for randomized instruments.

## Skip Popups
This patch skips certain popups to make the flow of the game better for an Archipelago.\
Recommended to use a patch to disable the star spawn cutscene.\

Also removes the need to be near various NPC star triggers, instead making them spawn as soon as Mario would be able to talk to them.
